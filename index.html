Sticonize

Drop an SVG — get it back redrawn freehand.

Hand-drawn SVG icons, in the browser

Sticonize redraws any SVG icon with a sketchy, hand-made stroke: shake, bowing, pen width, hatching, cross-hatch, zigzag, dots or solid fill. Everything runs locally in your browser — your files never leave it.

  • Drop an SVG file, paste SVG code, or search the Iconify icon library
  • Free vectorizer: trace a PNG, JPG or WEBP into a clean, editable SVG, then send it straight to the stylize tab
  • Six ready-made recipes: wireframe, classic hatch, thick scribble, dense charcoal, pointillist, clean ink
  • Export as SVG, PNG, a Vue component, a React component, or every icon at once as a ZIP
  • Dark and light themes, English, Portuguese and Dutch

Free, no signup, no watermark.
